Why it matters

Your team already uses AI. Your data should not leave to do it.

Today, employees paste internal documents into public chatbots to get quick answers. The answers sound confident but cite nothing, and company data leaves the building.

The problem is not whether to use AI. It is that answers need to come from your own data, and stay there.

How it works

Three steps to get started.

  1. Connect your knowledge

    Add internal documents and data sources to the knowledge base.

  2. Set the guardrails

    Choose models, who can use them, and what data is masked.

  3. Ask and automate

    Employees ask questions and build agents, with usage tracked per team.

Managed in one place: models, prompts, access and costs.

FAQ

Questions about PrivateGPT.

What is PrivateGPT?

PrivateGPT is a managed generative AI service for companies: AI answers grounded in your own data, with sources, kept private.

Who is PrivateGPT for?

Enterprises and public institutions that want employees to use AI on internal knowledge without sending data to public tools.

How is PrivateGPT different from public AI chatbots?

Public AI chatbots answer from general training data and keep what you paste. PrivateGPT answers from your documents, cites the source and applies your guardrails.

What do we need before we start?

The documents or data sources you want AI to answer from, and a list of teams who will use it.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the knowledge sources and teams you start with. Our team shares a plan after a consultation.

Can it connect with our other applications?

Yes: other applications can use PrivateGPT through its API & SDK.

How is our data kept secure?

Guardrails mask personal data, access is set per user and team, and activity is logged.

How do packages and pricing work?

Pricing depends on users, models and usage. Contact us for a proposal.
