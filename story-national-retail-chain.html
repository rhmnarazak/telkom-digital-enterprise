Context

Who they are

The chain runs 120 supermarkets and convenience stores across Java and Sumatra, serving neighbourhoods where it is often the closest place to buy daily groceries. Fresh produce and fast-moving goods make up most of its sales, so a shelf that stays empty for an afternoon is a sale that goes to the shop next door.

Regional operations managers look after 15 to 20 stores each. Their job is to keep shelves full and queues short, with store teams that change shifts twice a day and a head office that wants the same standard in every branch.

The network kept growing. But keeping every shelf and every queue in view got harder with each new store.

The challenge

Why the old way stopped working

Before the change, shelf checks happened on foot. Staff walked the aisles three times a day with a paper checklist, and most gaps were spotted at the evening count, when it was too late to restock from the back room. CCTV recorded every aisle, but the footage sat on a recorder in each store and was only opened after an incident.

The problem was not a lack of cameras. It was that no one could watch 120 stores at once, so the data the cameras captured never reached the people who could act on it. Out-of-stock items and long queues showed up in the sales report a week later.

By the time we saw the empty shelf in the report, the customer had already gone somewhere else.

Regional Operations Manager, National retail chain

Their goal was clear: Know about empty shelves and crowded tills while there is still time to fix them, in every store.

The solution

How IP Camera Management changed the work

The chain chose IP Camera Management because it worked with the IP cameras most stores already had and kept footage in the cloud instead of on local recorders. A pilot in 10 stores ran for six weeks before the rollout to the rest of the network.

The biggest change came from People Counting & Crowd Detection. Entrance and aisle cameras now count visitors and flag when an area gets crowded, so a store manager opens another till before the queue grows, and plans shifts around the real peaks instead of guesses.

We used to find empty shelves at the end of the day. Now the store team gets an alert while there is still time to restock.

Regional Operations Manager, National retail chain

Multi-site Deployment put all 120 stores in one view. Regional managers compare branches side by side and see which stores fall behind during the day, not at the end of the month.

When something looks wrong, Cloud Recording means the clip is already there. A manager opens it on a phone and shares it with the store team, without driving to the branch to pull footage off a recorder.

I check the crowd level on my phone before lunch and move one person to the tills. It takes a minute.

Store Manager, National retail chain

The stores' connections are watched by Network Management as a supporting service: when a branch link drops, Real-time Notifications reach IT before cameras go offline, so recordings and counts are not lost.

The results

What changed, in numbers

Out-of-stock time per storeBaseline30% less

The result was not only fewer empty shelves. Store meetings changed: instead of reviewing last week's sales, teams start the day with yesterday's visitor peaks and plan who restocks when.

Head office now compares stores on the same live numbers, and new branches are added to the same view on their first day.

We stopped managing stores from last week's report. Every regional manager now sees the same picture, at the same time.

Director of Retail Operations, National retail chain

What's next

Where they go from here

Next, the chain plans to bring smart lamps and door sensors into the same app with Smart Device Integration, and to extend Intruder Detection to its distribution centres. The goal is one view of every site it runs, which is what IP Camera Management is built for: cameras that alert you while it happens, not after.

FAQ

Questions about IP Camera Management.

How does IP Camera Management help retail stores?

It turns existing IP cameras into live signals: People Counting & Crowd Detection shows visitor peaks and crowding, and Cloud Recording keeps footage reachable from any device. In this story, the chain cut out-of-stock time by 30%.

Can it run across many stores?

Yes. Multi-site Deployment puts every location in one view. This chain manages 120 stores from one app.

Do we need new cameras?

Not always. The chain used the IP cameras most stores already had. Our team checks compatibility during a site survey.

How long does a rollout take?

It depends on the number of sites. This chain ran a six-week pilot in 10 stores before rolling out to the rest.
